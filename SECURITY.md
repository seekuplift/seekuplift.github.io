# Security Policy

## Reporting a vulnerability

Email **security@seekuplift.com**. Please do not open a public issue or post details publicly
before we have fixed the problem.

Include what you can:

- what is affected (the Android app, the web app, `app-api.seekuplift.com`, this website, or
  an on-chain program, with its address);
- steps to reproduce, or a proof of concept;
- the impact you expect, and any transaction signatures or wallet addresses involved.

We acknowledge reports as soon as we can and keep you posted until the issue is resolved.

## Scope

- The Seek Uplift Android app (`com.seekuplift.app`) and web app
- The Seek Uplift API (`app-api.seekuplift.com`) and update server (`updates.seekuplift.com`)
- `seekuplift.com`

Out of scope: third-party wallets, RPC providers and other services we use but do not
operate (report those to their vendors), denial-of-service and volumetric testing, social
engineering, and anything that touches other people's funds or accounts.

## Good faith

We will not pursue action against research done in good faith that stays in scope, avoids
privacy violations and service disruption, uses only your own accounts and funds, and gives
us reasonable time to fix the issue before disclosure.

The machine-readable contact is at
[`/.well-known/security.txt`](https://seekuplift.com/.well-known/security.txt).
