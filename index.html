Why Seek Uplift

Fees as fuel for something bigger.

Most launchpads stop at the chart. Here, every launch is tied to an outcome people can follow.

Fees with a purpose

Each coin backs a cause, an idea, or the Seek Uplift fund. A share of its fees flows there.

Paid on proof

Idea funding is released milestone by milestone, once the builder's proof is approved.

Optional transfer fees

Add a 1% to 10% transfer fee at launch, and funding keeps flowing on every transfer.

Built on SKR

Coins trade against SKR, the Seeker's own coin. Creators earn SKR from trades.

Ideas

Fund ideas, with accountability built in.

Back an app, a tool or a project worth building. Its coin's fees build a funding round, and every payout is tied to a milestone.

  • Builders post proof for each milestone
  • The fee router pays once the proof is approved
  • If a builder stops, the community can take over
Causes

Memes with impact.

Launch something fun that still does good. Pick the cause your coin backs, and part of its fees supports it for as long as people trade.

  • Back causes that no charity covers yet
  • Seek Uplift fund payouts are published with a reason
Creators

Rewards in SKR, and in your own coin.

Creators earn a share of trading fees in SKR. Turn on a transfer fee and you also collect your share in your own coin. Claim it all from one screen.
